Application Process

How to Apply

Complete the online application and provide the required financial documentation. Applicants should review all information carefully before submitting.

  1. 1

    Review Eligibility

    Confirm that your business meets the program's eligibility and documentation requirements.

  2. 2

    Complete the Application

    Provide your applicant information, business narrative, financial information, growth opportunity, use of funds, expected impact, and final statement.

  3. 3

    Upload Financial Documents

    Upload your complete 2024 and 2025 Profit & Loss statements and any required LARA documentation.

  4. 4

    Review & Submit

    Review your responses for accuracy and completeness, then submit the application before the deadline.

Applications must be submitted by September 30, 2026.
