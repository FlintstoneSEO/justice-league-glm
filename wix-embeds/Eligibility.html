Before You Apply

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must meet the program eligibility requirements and provide the required business and financial documentation before the application can proceed to competitive review.

01

Applicant Eligibility

Applicant must identify as Black / African American consistent with the program's eligibility requirements.

02

Operating History

The business must have been operating for a minimum of three years.

03

Annual Revenue

Business must have at least $20,000 in annual business revenue.

04

LARA Good Standing

Business must be registered and in good standing with the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs.

05

Tri-County Location

The business must be physically located in Ingham, Clinton, or Eaton County in Michigan.

06

Financial Records

Applicants must provide complete 2024 and 2025 Profit & Loss statements.
