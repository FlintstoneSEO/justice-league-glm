The Business Growth Grant is designed to provide capital to eligible African American-owned businesses that have historically experienced barriers to accessing traditional financing. Funding is intended to support meaningful, measurable business growth.
Each selected business will receive a $11,250 grant, subject to program terms and available funding. This is a grant, not a traditional loan.
Recipients must comply with all program requirements and any applicable reporting or documentation requirements.
Applicants must meet all program eligibility requirements, including:
  • Identify as Black/African American.
  • Have operated the business for at least 3 years.
  • Have at least $20,000 in annual business revenue.
  • Be registered and in good standing with LARA.
  • Be physically located in Ingham, Clinton, or Eaton County.
  • Provide 2024 and 2025 Profit & Loss statements.
The Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (LARA) maintains business-registration records in Michigan. For this grant, your business must be registered and meet the program requirement for being in good standing with LARA.
The statements help reviewers understand the financial performance and trajectory of the business, including revenue, expenses, profitability, and significant changes over time.
Submit your 2024 and 2025 Profit & Loss statements. Complete statements are preferred and should show revenue, expenses, and net income or loss. If you do not have formal financial statements, submit the most complete and accurate financial records available and contact info@justiceleagueglm.org before applying to discuss acceptable documentation.
Potential growth-related uses may include:
  • Equipment
  • Inventory or supplies
  • Technology
  • Marketing
  • Staffing
  • Facility improvements
  • Professional services
  • Increased production or service capacity
  • Expansion into new markets
  • Other approved business-growth investments

Growth outcomes may include increased revenue, new customers, expanded capacity, jobs created or retained, improved efficiency, or entry into new markets.
Eligible applications will be evaluated using established criteria that include the business narrative and value proposition, financial performance, growth opportunity, proposed use of grant funds, expected measurable impact, business capacity and financial management, and why the grant is needed now.

Before You Submit
  • Confirm that you meet the eligibility requirements.
  • Make sure your business is in good standing with LARA, if required.
  • Upload your 2024 Profit & Loss statement.
  • Upload your 2025 Profit & Loss statement.
  • Explain what your business does, who it serves, and its value proposition.
  • Clearly explain your specific growth opportunity.
  • Explain exactly how the $11,250 will be used and why each major expense is necessary.
  • Identify measurable outcomes expected within 12 months.
  • Make sure your proposed expenses and expected outcomes are realistic.
  • Submit all required documentation before the deadline.
