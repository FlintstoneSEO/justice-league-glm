Important Dates

Application Timeline

Follow the key dates below from application opening through review and recipient announcement.

August 30, 2026

Applications Open
Eligible businesses may begin submitting applications.

September 30, 2026

Application Deadline
All applications and required documents must be submitted.

October 1 – October 31, 2026

Application Review
Eligible applications are reviewed by the grant committee.

2026 Fall Celebration

Recipients Announced
Selected grant recipients are announced at the Fall Celebration.

Applicants should submit all required information and supporting documentation by the September 30, 2026 deadline to ensure the application can proceed to review.
