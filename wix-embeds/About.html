Building Black Economic Power

About the Growth Grant

African American-owned businesses are a vital part of the Greater Lansing economy, demonstrating resilience, entrepreneurship, innovation, and community leadership. Yet Black business owners continue to experience systemic barriers that can limit access to capital and opportunities for sustainable growth.

The Justice League of Greater Lansing Michigan created the Business Growth Grant through its Entrepreneurship Reparations pillar to provide eligible African American-owned businesses with capital that can strengthen operations and create measurable opportunities for expansion.

Grant funds are intended to help businesses invest in areas such as business infrastructure, marketing and branding, innovation, equipment, technology, staffing, increased capacity, and expansion into new markets.

By investing directly in Black-owned businesses, JLGLM seeks to help address historic barriers to capital while supporting job creation, business sustainability, economic opportunity, and stronger communities throughout the Greater Lansing area.

This grant is more than financial assistance. It is an investment in economic equity, sustainable business growth, community empowerment, and the long-term success of African American-owned businesses.
