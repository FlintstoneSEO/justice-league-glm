The Justice League of Greater Lansing Michigan proudly recognizes Stable Communities Foundation as the lead grantor supporting the 2026 Business Growth Grant initiative.

Through this investment, Stable Communities Foundation is helping expand access to capital for African American-owned businesses in the Greater Lansing area and supporting opportunities for sustainable growth, job creation, and stronger communities.
